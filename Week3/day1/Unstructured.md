delivery checker project. companies frequently discover that goods have been damaged or gone missing by the time a shipment reaches its destination, but they have no visibility into what happened during transit. without a way to verify the condition of the shipment along the route, it is difficult to identify where the damage occurred, who is responsible, and how to prevent it from happening again. delivery checker addresses this by giving companies a simple tool for verifying shipment condition from pickup to final delivery. features: employee dispatch to shipment location: assign an employee to travel to the pickup, transit checkpoint, or destination to physically inspect the goods. condition check-ins: the assigned employee logs the state of the shipment (e.g., intact, damaged, missing items) at each checkpoint before the shipment continues. photo/note capture: attach a photo and short note to each check-in as evidence. accounts: 3 types. 1. the manager, 2. the employee 3. the customer. manager has access to everything can manage the employee's clients and assign employee's client. employee has access to his/her clients shipment and documentation (photo, status etc). client can only check shipment and communicate with the employee. normal login. chat section: manager to have a chat with employee only and client to have a chat with employee only. employee adds check-in. if final delivery (complete shipment) checked, can't add check-ins after that. upload photo option that displays the photo in the timeline. customer requests a shipment providing pickup location, final destination, description of goods, and type (sensitive, etc). manager approves the requests. dark mode and light mode toggle. data stored in localstorage.
